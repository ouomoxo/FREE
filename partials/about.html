A colophon for a notebook that never was — what this is, how it was made, and where it leads.

What this is これは何か

A fan-made, non-commercial tribute to the fictional manga and anime Death Note, created by Tsugumi Ohba and Takeshi Obata. Everything on these pages is invention and interactive fiction: the notebook, its rules, the ledger you may keep. No real person is named, targeted, or harmed — nothing written here reaches beyond the screen.

Built with 作り

  • Zero dependencies. Hand-written HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no libraries.
  • No build step. The files you download are the files that run. Nothing is compiled or bundled.
  • No network. Not a single external request — no CDN, no fonts, no analytics. It works fully offline.
  • Your data stays yours. The Ledger and your cipher preferences live only in your own browser, via localStorage. Clear it and they are gone.
