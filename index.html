    03 · 規則

    How to Use It

    使い方

    The laws printed on the first leaves of the notebook — immutable, indifferent, and binding upon whoever should pick it up.

    DEATH NOTE 使用規則 HOW TO USE · XIV RULES

    1. The human whose name is written in this note shall die.

    2. This note will not take effect unless the writer has the subject’s face in their mind when writing the name. Those who merely share the same name are left unharmed.

    3. If the cause of death is written within 40 seconds of writing the person’s name, it will happen.

    4. If the cause of death is not specified, the person will simply die of a heart attack.

    5. After writing the cause of death, the details must be entered within the following 6 minutes and 40 seconds.

    6. A death takes effect only within human possibility. Suicide is a valid cause — every human is thought to hold the capacity for it.

    7. Should a written death be impossible, or would it claim lives beyond the one intended, the subject dies of a heart attack instead.

    8. You cannot kill a human over 124 years of age, nor one with fewer than 12 minutes of life left to live.

    9. A death once written cannot be undone. Crossing out a name or burning the page will not return the dead.

    10. The owner may trade half of their remaining lifespan for the eyes of a Shinigami — sight that reveals every human’s name and lifespan above their head.

    11. A Shinigami bound to a human may never tell that human the lifespan its eyes can see, whatever the reason.

    12. The owner recalls the deaths only while the note is theirs. Relinquish it, and every memory of the note is lost.

    13. A human who has used the note may go neither to Heaven nor to Hell. Beyond death waits only nothingness — 無.

    14. Whoever ceases to own the note must write a name within 13 days, or they will die. — a rule of dubious authorship, forged to shield the guilty.

    XIII 13

    These are the rules of a notebook that does not exist — drawn faithfully from the fiction Death Note. No name written here will ever come true.
